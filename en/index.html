FAQ

What people ask before the first call

How much does an AI consultation cost?

The first 20-minute call is free. A 90-minute architecture review is $500, a 60-minute mentoring session is $150, and an AI audit with a roadmap starts at $4,000 for two weeks. International clients get an invoice in USD or EUR.

Where should a company start with AI?

With an audit. We look at processes, data and product, choose two or three use cases with a measurable effect, estimate the cost of ownership and build a prototype of the top one. In two weeks you know what to build, what it costs and what to expect.

Which tasks fit LLMs and AI agents?

Customer support and internal assistants, search over documents (RAG), processing requests and emails, extracting data from documents, generating content and product listings, automating routine work in CRM and operations. If a task repeats and you have examples of correct answers, it can almost always be automated.

What does a fractional Head of AI do?

Owns AI strategy and architecture for a company that doesn't need a full-time hire yet: picks use cases, designs the LLM, RAG or agent layer, sets up evaluation and cost control, and guides the engineering team or vendor. I offer this as an ongoing engagement after an audit.

Can you review a RAG or agent system we already built?

Yes, that's the architecture review: retrieval quality, evaluation, cost per request, latency, reliability in production, and a 30-day plan with concrete steps. You get a written summary.

Which models and stacks do you work with?

GPT, Claude and Gemini, plus open-source models (Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek) deployed on-premise when data can't leave your infrastructure. Google ADK, LangGraph, vector databases, PyTorch, Hugging Face, AWS and GCP. The model is a design decision, not a given.
